MARC: But then you cross over into the other kingdom: Quantum Mechanics. This describes the universe on the subatomic scale—atoms, electrons, quarks, and the forces that govern them. SARAH: These two theories are both spectacularly successful in their own domains. General Relativity predicts the behavior of black holes and gravitational waves with staggering precision. MARC: And there are two extreme places in the universe where you absolutely *must* use both theories at the same time: the very center of a black hole, and the very beginning of the universe—the Big Bang. MARC: But String Theory has run into a major PR crisis in recent years, largely because it hasn't made any testable, experimental predictions that we can verify with our current technology.
