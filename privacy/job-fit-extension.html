Job Fit Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026. Encryption applies to version 1.0.4 and later. The Workday helper applies to version 1.0.6 and later.

Job Fit helps you compare job descriptions with your own resume and skills using your Gemini API key. It has no developer-operated backend, analytics, advertising, or account system.

Information you provide and save

The extension saves your resume Markdown, skills, optional Workday Skills / Tools list, Gemini API key, model preference, and a profile revision in Chrome's local extension storage. A resume may contain your name, contact details, employment history, education, and any other information you choose to include.

Successful checks save the job URL and title, compatibility score, summary, full explanation, model, check date, and profile revision as local checked-job history. AI explanations can mention information from your resume. History does not copy your full resume, API key, or full job description.

Profile and checked-job records, including job URLs and titles, are saved together in an encrypted vault, not Chrome sync. Job text, selected text, in-progress state, and errors are held temporarily in Chrome's memory-only extension session storage, scoped to the tab and URL. Tab data is removed when the tab closes; successful results also remain in the encrypted checked-job history.

Encryption and unlocking

The vault uses AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. A key is derived locally from your passphrase using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 with 600,000 iterations and a random salt. Each write uses a fresh random initialization vector. Your passphrase is never saved by the extension or sent to Google or the developer.

While unlocked, the encryption key is held only in Chrome's memory-only extension session storage, restricted to trusted extension contexts. Decrypted information also exists in extension memory and open interface fields. Lock vault now cancels running analyses, clears session data and the unlocked key, and clears open Job Fit interfaces. Fully quitting Chrome also ends the unlocked session. You must enter the passphrase again to access saved data. There is no passphrase recovery; you can delete local data and start over.

If upgrading from version 1.0.3 or earlier, existing unencrypted records remain until you create a vault or delete local data. Creating the vault encrypts existing records before removing the legacy plaintext records. This does not securely erase old disk blocks or backups. Original and exported resume Markdown files are outside the vault and are not encrypted by Job Fit. Encryption cannot protect against someone using your unlocked browser or a compromised device.

Reading job pages

Opening Job Fit on a page, or requesting selected text, allows it to read the active page's job metadata and relevant page text. The right-click command uses the text you selected. Job Fit does not run always-on content scripts or collect your general browsing history. It only adds URLs to persistent history for jobs you successfully score.

Workday Skills / Tools entry

You may save a separate list of Workday skill/tool labels in your encrypted profile. Opening the helper explicitly loads that list into a temporary helper on the active HTTPS myworkdayjobs.com or myworkdaysite.com page. The website can read helper text and entered values. Each Search & add click enters one label into the input you select, waits four seconds, and clicks only a single exact visible suggestion that is not already marked selected. Missing or ambiguous matches require your action. The helper does not click application Next or Submit.

The helper does not provide your resume, Gemini API key, or vault passphrase to Workday. The separate Workday list is not sent to Gemini by the extension. Information entered on Workday may be autosaved or processed by Workday and the employer under their policies; review the application before submitting.

The helper's tab URL and an authorization token are kept temporarily in memory-only extension session storage. The list also exists in the helper's page memory while open. Closing the helper cancels its pending selection. Locking, saving the profile, or clearing data/history invalidates helper authorization and attempts to close open helpers; tab closure removes its temporary state. If a changed page prevents cleanup, refresh or close that tab. These actions do not retract values already entered or information Workday or an employer may have received.

Information sent to Google

When you click Score compatibility or Recheck compatibility, your saved resume, skills, and reviewed job description are sent directly over HTTPS to Google's Gemini API at generativelanguage.googleapis.com. Your API key is sent to Google as an authentication header. Remove personal details you do not want to share before scoring.

Loading available models sends your API key to Google to request model metadata. Test connection sends the key and a small sample prompt to verify JSON generation; it does not send your resume, skills, or job description. Model discovery can also run as part of a scoring request. Merely saving your profile or viewing history does not send your resume to Google.

Google's processing, retention, logging, model-improvement practices, availability, and charges depend on your account, service tier, and applicable terms. Review the Gemini API terms and Google Privacy Policy before sending personal information. Deleting data in Job Fit does not delete data Google may already have processed or retained.

Purpose and sharing

Job Fit uses this information only to provide the job-fit analysis, history, and optional Workday skills entry you request. The developer does not receive your profile, API key, job descriptions, or history through an extension server. The extension does not sell personal data or use it for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated purposes. AI scores are estimates for your own review, not hiring decisions or probabilities.

Retention and your controls

Your local profile and checked-job history remain until you clear them. You can edit your resume, skills, Workday Skills / Tools, model preference, or API key in Profile & settings. Clear checked jobs removes history and session job text/results while preserving the profile. Delete local data removes the profile, API key, history, and session results. Both clearing actions cancel running analyses and stop Workday helpers. Delete local data also removes the vault and its unlocked key; Clear checked jobs keeps the vault unlocked. Uninstalling the extension also removes its Chrome-managed local storage, subject to Chrome and device backup behavior.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the developer contact provided on Job Fit's Chrome Web Store listing. If the extension's data practices change, this policy and the store disclosures will be updated.

Visiting this policy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not load analytics scripts, advertising, or third-party fonts. GitHub may process technical information about visits, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting this page does not send your extension profile or job history to this website.
