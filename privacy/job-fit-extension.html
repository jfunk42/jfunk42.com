Job Fit Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Job Fit helps you compare job descriptions with your own resume and skills using your Gemini API key. It has no developer-operated backend, analytics, advertising, or account system.

Information you provide and save

The extension saves your resume Markdown, skills, Gemini API key, model preference, and a profile revision in Chrome's local extension storage. A resume may contain your name, contact details, employment history, education, and any other information you choose to include.

Successful checks save the job URL and title, compatibility score, summary, full explanation, model, check date, and profile revision as local checked-job history. AI explanations can mention information from your resume. History does not copy your full resume, API key, or full job description.

Job text, selected text, in-progress state, and errors are stored temporarily in extension session storage. Session data is scoped to the tab and URL and is removed when the tab closes or the browser session ends. Successful results also remain in local checked-job history.

This data is not stored using Chrome sync and is not encrypted by the extension. Anyone with sufficient access to your device or browser profile may be able to access it.

Reading job pages

Opening Job Fit on a page, or requesting selected text, allows it to read the active page's job metadata and relevant page text. The right-click command uses the text you selected. Job Fit does not run always-on content scripts or collect your general browsing history. It stores URLs only for jobs you successfully score.

Information sent to Google

When you click Score compatibility or Recheck compatibility, your saved resume, skills, and reviewed job description are sent directly over HTTPS to Google's Gemini API at generativelanguage.googleapis.com. Your API key is sent to Google as an authentication header. Remove personal details you do not want to share before scoring.

Loading available models sends your API key to Google to request model metadata. Test connection sends the key and a small sample prompt to verify JSON generation; it does not send your resume, skills, or job description. Model discovery can also run as part of a scoring request. Merely saving your profile or viewing history does not send your resume to Google.

Google's processing, retention, logging, model-improvement practices, availability, and charges depend on your account, service tier, and applicable terms. Review the Gemini API terms and Google Privacy Policy before sending personal information. Deleting data in Job Fit does not delete data Google may already have processed or retained.

Purpose and sharing

Job Fit uses this information only to provide the job-fit analysis and history you request. The developer does not receive your profile, API key, job descriptions, or history through an extension server. The extension does not sell personal data or use it for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated purposes. AI scores are estimates for your own review, not hiring decisions or probabilities.

Retention and your controls

Your local profile and checked-job history remain until you clear them. You can edit your resume, skills, model preference, or API key in Profile & settings. Clear checked jobs removes history and session job text/results while preserving the profile. Delete local data removes the profile, API key, history, and session results. Both clearing actions cancel running analyses. Uninstalling the extension also removes its Chrome-managed local storage, subject to Chrome and device backup behavior.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the developer contact provided on Job Fit's Chrome Web Store listing. If the extension's data practices change, this policy and the store disclosures will be updated.

Visiting this policy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not load analytics scripts, advertising, or third-party fonts. GitHub may process technical information about visits, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting this page does not send your extension profile or job history to this website.
